TIAGo Pro right arm, MPC reaching on the Jetson
6 s loop

GPU-accelerated MPC on a real robot arm

Master's thesis, KIT · CUDA/C++, Python, ROS 2, Jetson AGX Orin · 2026
project page thesis PDF merged GATO PRs

I ported the GPU trajectory optimizer GATO to the right arm of a TIAGo Pro and ran it as a model predictive controller on the robot's embedded NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, in simulation and on the physical arm. Solve times allow about 150 control updates per second, and three fixes I found along the way were merged into GATO.

Solo · Python, JavaScript, Pyodide · 2020–2026

ReSTIR real-time lighting

Course project, team of 2 · C++, OpenGL, GLSL · 2024–2025

The method of Bitterli et al. (2020) for direct lighting from many area lights, built for KIT's practical course Real-Time Computer Graphics. My share was the sampling and reservoir pipeline: RIS, streaming RIS, temporal reuse and alias-table light sampling in GLSL compute shaders.

Type system meets theorem prover

Bachelor's thesis, KIT · Java, Checker Framework, KeY · 2023
code thesis PDF

Information-flow control for Java: the type checker SFlow checks most methods automatically and generates JML contracts for the theorem prover KeY where it fails. I extended the type system with rules for implicit flows and method types and proved the combined system sound.
